
Dear students, today in class we have determined an element, and how elements are arranged  in the periodic table (in  accordance to their atomic masses, atomic  numbers and common chemical and physical properties). Elements known by now are 118. Every element consists of the smallest particles - atoms. Atoms of the same element are the same, atoms of two different elements are different. Combining together through chemical bond  elements form compound. Compounds can present as neutral molecules or charged ions (cations and anions) and formed from two or more elements. We then have introduced  the Dalton atomic theory, the law of mass preservation and have been discussed on subatomic  particles discovery: electrons and protons (incide nucleus).
