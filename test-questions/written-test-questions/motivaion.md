# Why would like to study in Italy?
=>
Studying in Italy would provide an exceptional opportunity for someone with interests spanning computer science, mathematics, physics, geography, and fine art. Here are several reasons why:

Renowned Computer Science Programs: Italy boasts prestigious universities offering high-quality computer science programs, such as the University of Bologna and Politecnico di Milano. These institutions enable students to dive deep into their field while providing access to cutting-edge research facilities and collaborative projects. Moreover, being immersed in Italy's rich technological hub will expose you to innovative startups and companies, fostering valuable networking opportunities.
Mathematical and Physical Sciences Excellence: Italian universities offer rigorous mathematical and physical sciences curricula that cater to your keen interest in those fields. World-renowned centers like Scuola Normale Superiore di Pisa or Sant'Anna School of Advanced Studies attract brilliant minds from around the globe, creating a vibrant intellectual community where groundbreaking discoveries occur regularly.
Geographical Perspective: Given your enthusiasm for geography, there isn't a better place than Italy to learn more about cultural diversity and human interactions with various environments. With its unique blend of history, breathtaking landscapes, and regional differences, Italy offers endless possibilities for exploration and understanding. Additionally, many Italian universities incorporate environmental studies within their curriculum, enabling you to expand on your existing knowledge base.
Fine Arts Renaissance: As a lover of fine art, Italy provides unparalleled inspiration due to its historical significance during the Renaissance period. You can visit countless museums, galleries, and architectural wonders that showcase masterpieces created by legendary artists throughout time. By living amidst this artistic legacy, you'll enhance your creative process and develop a profound appreciation for visual storytelling.
Cultural Immersion: Living and studying in Italy exposes you to a multitude of languages, traditions, customs, and cuisines. Embracing this culturally rich environment enables personal growth and broadens one's perspective—an essential aspect of becoming a well-rounded individual prepared for success in today's globalized society.
Network Building Opportunities: Engaging with local communities, attending workshops, conferences, or participating in internships allows you to build connections with professionals working across industries. Such relationships may lead to future job prospects or collaborative ventures, further solidifying your status as an informed, engaged citizen of the world.

# Which subject do you feel hard and how do you deal with it? 
=> 
Chemistry
When it comes to challenging subjects, Organic Chemistry stands out for me. While I excel in Inorganic Chemistry and thrive on understanding atomic particles and metallurgy, Organic Chemistry has always been a hurdle. Concepts like valence bonds, s-p bonds, and others seemed like a nightmare to grasp initially. However, I approached this challenge by seeking guidance from my chemistry teacher. Their valuable advice was to relate these abstract concepts to real-life examples.

Through innovative teaching methods, such as using 3D structures of chemical compounds like benzene and illustrating their formation from ethyne, my teacher made the subject more tangible and comprehensible. This approach transformed my understanding of topics like the process of cyclic polymerization. Since then, I've adopted a strategy of visualizing and relating concepts rather than merely memorizing them. This shift in approach has not only helped me overcome my difficulties in Organic Chemistry but has also made the subject more engaging and enjoyable for me.
