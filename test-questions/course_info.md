Which subject do you feel hard and how do you deal with it? 
=> 
Chemistry
When it comes to challenging subjects, Organic Chemistry stands out for me. While I excel in Inorganic Chemistry and thrive on understanding atomic particles and metallurgy, Organic Chemistry has always been a hurdle. Concepts like valence bonds, s-p bonds, and others seemed like a nightmare to grasp initially. However, I approached this challenge by seeking guidance from my chemistry teacher. Their valuable advice was to relate these abstract concepts to real-life examples.

Through innovative teaching methods, such as using 3D structures of chemical compounds like benzene and illustrating their formation from ethyne, my teacher made the subject more tangible and comprehensible. This approach transformed my understanding of topics like the process of cyclic polymerization. Since then, I've adopted a strategy of visualizing and relating concepts rather than merely memorizing them. This shift in approach has not only helped me overcome my difficulties in Organic Chemistry but has also made the subject more engaging and enjoyable for me.
